First public release of **Vanity**, client-side armor cosmetics for Minecraft **26.3** (Fabric).

Wear diamond, show netherite. Your real armor and its protection don't change; only the look does, and only you see it.

### Install
1. Fabric Loader **0.20.1+**, **Fabric API** for 26.3, Java 25.
2. Drop `vanity-0.10.0.jar` into `mods/`.
3. In game press **V** to open the wardrobe (rebindable under Controls → Vanity).

### Features
- **Armor looks**: any armor material per slot or as a whole set. Resource packs apply to the swapped armor, and
  your real armor's enchant glint and trims carry over.
- **SkyBlock / custom items**: **Held** and **Worn** save the exact item (name, dye, player-head skin, custom data).
- **Inventory icons** show the look on the armor you're wearing; tooltips add a "Vanity look" line.
- **Hide when unequipped** (default) or keep the look; **Invisible** hides a slot entirely.
- **Style**: glint Like real / On / Off, leather dye with color slider and hex, **Opacity** (see-through armor),
  **Glow** (bright in the dark), **Trim glow** (soft glow on trims and designs).
- **Trims**: keep your own, none, or any vanilla pattern + material.
- **Designs**: custom trims, up to 3 trim patterns stacked, each any color.
- **Runes**: 18 particle effects in 6 shapes (halo, orbit, spiral, wings, aura, trail), with color, size, detail,
  length and speed. Visible in third person; optional in first person.
- **All tab**: change every armor piece at once. Rune shape "Per piece" keeps a halo on the head, orbit on the chest,
  spiral on the legs and a trail at the feet.
- **Glass wardrobe UI** with dropdowns, live player preview and a turnable view.
- Non-armor items (like a glass block on your head) are fitted to the armor shape automatically.

Settings are saved in `config/vanity.json`.
