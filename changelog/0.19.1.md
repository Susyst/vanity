**Vanity 0.19.1**: client-side armor cosmetics for Minecraft **26.3** (Fabric).

Wear diamond, show netherite. Your real armor and its protection don't change; only the look does, and only you see it.

### New in 0.19.1
- Ability effects now read the text above your hotbar however the server sends it (both kinds Hypixel can use).
- Swing trails curve into a crescent in first person instead of looking like a straight line.

### New in 0.19.0
- **Swing trails** (Hand → Swing): a trail where your weapon swings, in first and third person. Nine styles (Color,
  Sparkle, Flame, Soul fire, Frost, Electric, Magic, Void, Rainbow), four shapes (Diagonal, Flat, Overhead, Combo),
  size, amount and an optional whoosh. Weapons only, or every swing.
- **SkyBlock ability effects** (Hand → Abilities): teleports (Aspect of the End / Void, etherwarp, Wither Impact) leave
  a trail from where you were to where you land, with an implosion for Wither Impact; every other ability that costs
  mana sends out a ring. Read from the mana cost above your hotbar, so it works with any item.

### New in 0.18.0
- **Removed Vanity's own armor designs** (Frostforged, Obsidian Knight, Ember, Verdant, Royal Guard, Void Walker,
  Sakura, Circuit) and their 3D pieces, along with the **3D** button on the Look page. A slot that was set to one of
  them shows your real armor again.
- **Your own armor stays**: put `<name>.png` (and optionally `<name>_leggings.png` and `<name>_glow.png`) in
  `config/vanity/armors`; the look list's "+ Your own armor..." opens the folder.

### New in 0.14.1
- Dropdown lists are solid, so the buttons behind them don't show through.

### New in 0.14.0
- **Your own armor**: drop a 64x32 armor PNG in `config/vanity/armors` (the look list's "+ Your own armor..." opens it,
  with a template and an example) and wear it.
- **Hit & kill effects** (Hand tab): particles where you hit something, and an effect where something you hit dies:
  souls, lightning, fireworks, blood, hearts, totem, explosion, rainbow ring or void. Color, size, amount and sounds;
  **Try it** previews them.

### New in 0.13.1
- The item picker is fully opaque, long status lines fit the panel, and the Look page shows a short SkyBlock hint.

### New in 0.13.0
- **Fix**: a skinned item you pick up in an inventory keeps its skin on the cursor instead of switching back to its
  real texture.
- **Grab key (K)**: hover any item in any inventory, chest, menu or the Auction House and press K to save it, with all
  its SkyBlock data, for use as a look.
- **Pick**: a new item picker on the Look page and Hand tab shows your grabbed items and your own items with their real
  (texture pack) textures; click one to use it. **New skin...** picks the item to skin the same way.
- **SkyBlock ids as looks**: type `sb:HYPERION` (or just `HYPERION`) to show an item you don't own. Texture packs and
  mods that texture SkyBlock items by their id show it.

### New in 0.12.0
- **Item skins** (new **Hand** tab): make the item you hold look like another item, like a Hyperion shown as a Dark
  Claymore. Works in first and third person, the hotbar and inventory, with texture packs. SkyBlock items match by their
  SkyBlock id; other items by type. Tooltips add a "Vanity skin" line.
- **Animated designs and glow on any armor**: design colors can be Rainbow, Pulse or Shimmer, and glow can Breathe,
  Flicker or glow in shifting rainbow colors. One Speed slider per piece.
- **Auto-wear outfits**: pick an outfit to put on automatically whenever you join a server or singleplayer world.

### New in 0.11.0
- **Outfits**: save all four slots as an outfit (looks, dye, trims, designs, runes) on the new Outfits page, then press
  **N** in game to switch to the next one. Rename, update or delete outfits; up to 12.
- **Animated dye** for leather looks: **Rainbow** cycles every color, **Pulse** fades your color darker and back,
  **Shimmer** sways its hue. Speed slider. Also animates on your inventory icons.
- **Reactive runes**: a burst of your rune when you get hit, and twice as dense, bigger and faster while sprinting.
  Set per piece with **React** (Hit / Sprint / Both / Off).
- Long button labels are cut with "…" instead of spilling out on small screens.

### Install
1. Fabric Loader **0.20.1+**, **Fabric API** for 26.3, Java 25.
2. Drop `vanity-0.19.1.jar` into `mods/`.
3. In game press **V** to open the wardrobe (rebindable under Controls → Vanity).

### Everything else
- **Armor looks**: any armor material per slot or as a whole set. Resource packs apply to the swapped armor, and
  your real armor's enchant glint and trims carry over.
- **SkyBlock / custom items**: **Held** and **Worn** save the exact item (name, dye, player-head skin, custom data).
- **Inventory icons** show the look on the armor you're wearing; tooltips add a "Vanity look" line.
- **Hide when unequipped** (default) or keep the look; **Invisible** hides a slot entirely.
- **Style**: glint, leather dye, **Opacity** (see-through armor), **Glow** (bright in the dark), **Trim glow**.
- **Trims**: keep your own, none, or any vanilla pattern + material. **Designs**: up to 3 trim patterns, any colors.
- **Runes**: 18 particle effects in 6 shapes (halo, orbit, spiral, wings, aura, trail).
- **All tab** changes every armor piece at once. Glass wardrobe UI with dropdowns and a live preview.

Settings are saved in `config/vanity.json`.
